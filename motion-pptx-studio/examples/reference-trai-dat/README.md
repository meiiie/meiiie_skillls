# Reference: "Trái Đất" (22 slides, contact sheet only)
This is a quality yardstick from a deck Darren showed. Nothing in it is a template.

What it shows:
- **Per-chapter grounds:** night navy, cream paper, lava orange, ocean blue.
- **A real picture behind every slide:** full-bleed NASA photos, or flat editorial
  illustrations (atmosphere layers, a tectonics section, a core cutaway, a clock dial).
- **Each hero number has its own picture.**
- **Three type voices at strong scale contrast:** serif, heavy condensed and mono.
- **One quiet motif:** a thin orange rail with ticks.

Match its level of care, then invent your own direction for your topic
(principles/examples-are-a-yardstick-not-a-mold.md).
