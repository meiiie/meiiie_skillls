# Art directions (v5.3 anti-template phase)

Three different directions were sketched as thumbnails (design/directions.jpg) before the storyboard.

## Direction A — Bảng điều hành
- ground: cool light paper
- palette: #F5F7FA #0E7C6B #0F1722 #1F3A5F
- type: Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter
- family: boardroom-data
- composition: one headline figure huge on the left, a native bar chart that grows on the right
- motion: bars grow from the baseline; the lit bar morphs
- signature visual: the berth bar that splits into the new berth

## Direction B — Bản tin giấy
- ground: warm paper
- palette: #F3EEE4 #B0321E #1B1815
- type: Fraunces + Inter
- family: paper-editorial
- composition: claim in a serif headline, one line chart on a hairline axis
- motion: the hairline draws, then the line
- signature visual: the vermilion margin mark

## Direction C — Sơ đồ bến đêm
- ground: dark navy plan paper
- palette: #0E141C #E8A33D #E6EDF3
- type: IBM Plex Sans + Mono
- family: blueprint
- composition: the quay drawn as a technical plan, berths lit one by one
- motion: the quay outline traces, berths grow
- signature visual: the new berth drawn last

## Decision
- chosen: A
- why: a lender reads figures first: one sourced number per slide and a bar that grows into the new berth, in the calm boardroom register the brief asks for
