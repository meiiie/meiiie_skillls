# Example: "Bưu điện ánh sáng" v2 (universe, 30 slides, built with v6)
A lightweight copy: art direction, board and contact sheets only. No deck, films or images are
included.

- **Concept:** every sky image is a late letter. The deck's palette "redshifts" chapter by
  chapter.
- **Motif:** a lookback rail on the right edge (log light-travel time).
- **Type:** Fraunces, Barlow Condensed, IBM Plex Mono and Plex Sans.
- **Films:** 4 (3 Manim, 1 pure-Python canvas).

Self-score is ≈ 7.9 against ≈ 8.6 for the reference. The weak points are listed in
critique-log.md. Use it to see how a board and critique lead to a rebuild. Do not copy its
palettes, rail or layouts.

| file | what |

| board.jpg, board-critique.md | the board made BEFORE building, and its critique against the reference |
| contact-1/2/3.jpg | the final 30 slides (LibreOffice render) |
