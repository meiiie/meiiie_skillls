# Board critique vs the "Trái Đất" reference (22/10/2026)

What the reference does that v1 did not: per-chapter grounds; a real picture or flat illustration
behind every slide; every hero number given a picture; three type voices (serif, heavy condensed,
mono) at very different scales; one quiet UI motif (orange rail + ticks).

Board v2 check:
- Palettes: 7 themes, ordered so the deck redshifts. That's an *argument*, not decoration (better
  than the reference here). Risk: 14 of 30 tiles are dark (night/dark/gold), and the first 3 are all
  navy. Fix: the agenda becomes six full-height bands in the chapter colours (the palette shown as
  content), and the cover/hook are photographs, not flat navy.
- Hero colour: v1 used red for every hero. v2 uses the chapter accent (vermilion / lavender /
  crimson / gold / amber / vermilion) so heroes change colour with the chapter, like the reference.
- Compositions: 9 full-bleed or split photos, 4 films, 13 flat illustrations/diagrams, 4 typographic
  (agenda, board, close, credits). No two neighbours share a layout except the film pairs. The
  reference has 8 photo slides out of 22, so we are close.
- Openers: all 5 "big-number+illus" tiles must not look alike. Each gets its own geometry: spiral
  (c1), horizontal ruler (c2), stretched envelope (c3), centred hex mirror (c4), corner cutaway (c5),
  pie wedge (c6).
- Motif: the rail means something (light-travel time, log scale) rather than a page-progress bar.
  Its labels must stay ≥10 pt and never collide with content: content stops at x 12.15 in.
- Type: Fraunces + Barlow Condensed + Plex Mono + Plex Sans. The condensed numerals let heroes go to
  150–200 pt without overflowing (v1's serif heroes maxed out at 132 pt).
- Weakest areas to watch: the dot-field and board slides could read as data dumps, so keep the text
  to ≤ 2 lines. Films were designed on cream and need re-rendering on their chapter grounds.
