# paper-editorial — warm paper, ink serif, one vermilion mark

**Use for** essays, reports, research, policy, journalism and data stories that need to read like a quality magazine; also history.
**Palette** ground `#F3EEE4`, second ground `#1B1815`, surface `#FBF8F2`, ink `#1B1815`, muted `#5C544B`, hairline `#D3C9B8`, accent `#B0321E`, second voice `#21505F` (from the pack, assets/styles/paper-editorial.json).
**Fonts** Fraunces (display) · Inter (text) · Inter (labels).
**Routing fit** (route.py reads it from the pack): domains history 0.7, economy 0.7, science 0.6, business 0.5, culture 0.5, heritage 0.5, education 0.5, health 0.5, climate 0.5, travel 0.4; tones calm, warm, precise, editorial, serious.

## Layout grammar
- Warm paper ground, an ink-dark second ground for chapter breaks; ONE vermilion mark, a deep teal second voice for data.
- Fraunces serif display for the claim, Inter text; a running head (`!!running`) and a top bar keep the magazine rhythm.
- Asymmetric columns: claim in the left third, one chart or photo in the right two thirds; captions under pictures, sources in the chrome.
- Charts are drawn on one hairline that becomes the axis of every chart in the deck.

## Signature motion
- 1.3 s tempo; `wipe` along the hairline, `rise` for paragraphs, Morph between chart states.
- Signature: a vermilion margin mark that walks down the page with the argument.
- The essay's key number set huge in the serif, parked in the margin between chapters.

## Avoid
neon, glossy gradients, cartoon outlines, more than two type families, centred title-only slides.

## Routing rejects it when the deck is about
- none
