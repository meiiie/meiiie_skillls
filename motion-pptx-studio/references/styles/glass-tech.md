# glass-tech — midnight mesh gradient, frosted cards, cyan-violet glow (experimental)

**Use for** AI, SaaS and fintech product pitches and future-facing keynotes where the "glass" look is expected.
**Palette** ground `#0A0F24`, second ground `#141B3D`, surface `#FFFFFF`, ink `#E9ECFF`, muted `#BCC3EA`, hairline `#3A4378`, accent `#7CF7FF`, second voice `#B18CFF` (from the pack, assets/styles-experimental/glass-tech.json).
**Fonts** Lexend (display) · Inter (text) · JetBrains Mono (labels).
**Routing fit** (route.py reads it from the pack): domains technology 1, software 0.8, business 0.5, aerospace 0.4, economy 0.2; tones bold, cinematic.

## Layout grammar
- Midnight mesh-gradient ground; frosted translucent cards (white surface at low alpha) with rounded corners (0.12 in).
- Lexend display, Inter text, JetBrains Mono labels; cyan is the signal, violet the second voice.
- A looping procedural aurora film may sit behind the cards; product screens float on soft long shadows.

## Signature motion
- 1.2 s tempo; `rise` and `float` for cards, `pulse` loop on the key card, `zoom`/`vortex` transitions used sparingly.
- Signature: a glowing orb orbits the product screen (orbit path, repeat).

## Avoid
heritage (frosted glass is the SaaS look), dense data tables, more than one glow per slide. Experimental: route to it only when the brief asks for this look.

## Routing rejects it when the deck is about
- heritage: frosted cyan-violet glass panels are the SaaS look
