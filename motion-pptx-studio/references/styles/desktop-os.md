# desktop-os — wallpaper gradient, app windows, a dock (experimental)

**Use for** class presentations, member intros, app ideas and tutorials for young audiences: a playful fictional UI.
**Palette** ground `#5B7CFA`, second ground `#EEF1F8`, surface `#FFFFFF`, ink `#1D2433`, muted `#26304A`, hairline `#DCE2EE`, accent `#FF6B5B`, second voice `#5B7CFA` (from the pack, assets/styles-experimental/desktop-os.json).
**Fonts** Plus Jakarta Sans (display) · Be Vietnam Pro (text) · JetBrains Mono (labels).
**Routing fit** (route.py reads it from the pack): domains youth 1, education 0.7, software 0.5; tones playful.

## Layout grammar
- A wallpaper-gradient ground; content sits in app windows with title bars and traffic dots; a dock and a menu bar as chrome.
- Plus Jakarta Sans display, Be Vietnam Pro text, JetBrains Mono for the clock and labels.
- Always a fictional UI: never a real OS or brand; one window in focus per slide, the others dimmed.

## Signature motion
- 0.9 s tempo; windows `zoom` out of their dock icon (Morph from the icon), notification toasts fly in top right.
- Signature: dock icons link to chapter slides (an interactive menu).

## Avoid
heritage, aerospace and economics (a fictional desktop turns the subject into an app or a game). Experimental.

## Routing rejects it when the deck is about
- heritage: a fictional UI pastiche frames its subject as an app
- aerospace: window chrome and a dock make real hardware look like a mock-up
- economy: a fictional desktop makes official statistics look like a game
