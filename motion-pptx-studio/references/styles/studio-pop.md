# studio-pop — cream and sun-yellow, ink outlines, hard shadows

**Use for** education, workshops, youth, community events and festivals: friendly, loud, hands-on.
**Palette** ground `#FFF4DE`, second ground `#FFD447`, surface `#FFFFFF`, ink `#1C1A35`, muted `#55507A`, hairline `#EADBB8`, accent `#E8461F`, second voice `#2E6BFF` (from the pack, assets/styles/studio-pop.json).
**Fonts** Bricolage Grotesque (display) · Be Vietnam Pro (text) · Be Vietnam Pro (labels).
**Routing fit** (route.py reads it from the pack): domains education 1, youth 1, event 0.7, food 0.5; tones playful, energetic, warm, friendly.

## Layout grammar
- Cream ground, sun-yellow second ground; tomato red and cobalt as the two voices; everything outlined in ink.
- Bricolage Grotesque display, Be Vietnam Pro text; sticker cards with hard ink shadows (blur 0, 6 pt offset).
- Big words, few of them; one illustration or cut-out photo per slide; tilted stickers are allowed.
- A hand-drawn underline under the one word that matters.

## Signature motion
- 1.1 s tempo; `zoom`, `teeter` and `rise` entrances, sticker slap-on and peel-off between slides.
- Signature: a tomato-red dot that bounces from slide to slide and becomes the bullet, the pin and the full stop.

## Avoid
serious subjects (aerospace hardware, heritage, inflation and debt: cartoon outlines trivialise them), thin hairlines, long paragraphs.

## Routing rejects it when the deck is about
- aerospace: sun-yellow cartoon outlines shrink real hardware to toys
- heritage: cartoon outlines trivialise what they draw
- economy: cartoon outlines and sun yellow trivialise inflation and debt
