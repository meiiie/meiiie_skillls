# papercut-collage — kraft ground, layered torn paper, warm craft palette (experimental)

**Use for** food, travel, children, community and storytelling decks: handmade warmth, landscapes built in layers.
**Palette** ground `#EFE2C8`, second ground `#2F5D46`, surface `#FBF6EC`, ink `#2B2118`, muted `#5A4834`, hairline `#D8C6A3`, accent `#B53A1F`, second voice `#2F5D46` (from the pack, assets/styles-experimental/papercut-collage.json).
**Fonts** Baloo 2 (display) · Be Vietnam Pro (text) · Patrick Hand (labels).
**Routing fit** (route.py reads it from the pack): domains food 1, travel 0.8, culture 0.8, youth 0.7, heritage 0.5; tones warm, playful.

## Layout grammar
- Kraft-paper ground, forest-green second ground; torn-paper freeform shapes in layers with soft drop shadows.
- Baloo 2 display, Be Vietnam Pro text, Patrick Hand labels; tomato red and forest green, a marigold lit colour.
- Photos as cut-outs pasted on paper; scenes built from 3-5 paper layers at different depths.

## Signature motion
- 1.1 s tempo; layers `rise` one after another (parallax via Morph), `peeloff`/`pagecurl` between chapters.
- Signature: a cut-out sun that slowly spins (spin loop).

## Avoid
aerospace, finance and economics (torn kraft paper makes hardware and figures look homemade). Experimental.

## Routing rejects it when the deck is about
- aerospace: torn kraft paper is handmade warmth
- finance: craft paper and torn edges make figures look homemade
- economy: craft paper and torn edges make figures look homemade
