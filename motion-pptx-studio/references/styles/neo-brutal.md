# neo-brutal — stickers, thick ink, hard shadows

**Use for** recipes, how-tos, workshops, youth and community talks, playful product chapters.
**Palette** bone `#F3EEE3` or lime `#C5F25A` grounds, ink `#111111`, orange `#FF5A36` (text on bone uses `#D9431F`
for 4.5:1), electric blue `#3D5AFE` sparingly.
**Fonts** Dela Gothic One (display) · Space Grotesk (text) · Space Mono (tags).

## Layout grammar
- A poster, not a grid of cards: one giant numeral or word (230 pt) on the left, photos as tilted stickers
  (−5° … +4°) with 3 pt ink borders and a 9 px hard shadow, overlapping each other a little.
- Tags are black or orange strips with mono caps (`HÀ NỘI · 1946`), rotated with their sticker.
- Steps are four equal blocks with a 60 pt numeral, a one-word verb and ≤ 15 words; the last step in orange.
- Chrome is a black badge with `#n` bottom right; nothing else.

## Signature motion
- Stickers `drop` in, 300 ms apart; tags `fly` from the right after them; a block `zoom`s in last.
- A sticker the audience asks about `teeter`s on click (`trigger`: its own name).
- Steps: the first block drops on arrival, the next ones one per click (`click`: 1, 2, 3).
- Transitions `cover` or `push` (paper being slid on a desk).

## Avoid
Soft shadows, gradients, more than three tilted objects, tilted body text.
