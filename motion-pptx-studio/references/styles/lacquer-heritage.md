# lacquer-heritage — dark lacquer, gold hairlines, a calligraphic serif

**Use for** heritage, craft, ceremony and museum talks where the object is old, made by hand and handled with respect (ca trù, lacquer, temples, court music).
**Palette** ground `#140B08`, second ground `#5E1410`, surface `#24150F`, ink `#F3E6CC`, muted `#BFA983`, hairline `#6E5A3E`, accent `#D9B26A`, second voice `#EBCB86` (from the pack, assets/styles/lacquer-heritage.json).
**Fonts** Cormorant (display) · Josefin Sans (text) · Josefin Sans (labels).
**Routing fit** (route.py reads it from the pack): domains heritage 1, culture 1, history 0.7; tones solemn, luxurious, warm.

## Layout grammar
- A dark lacquer ground with a red second ground for chapter openers; a thin gold frame (`!!frame`) holds every page like a museum mount.
- Cormorant display set large and light, Josefin Sans for text and labels in tracked caps; never more than one gold accent per slide.
- The object photographed on dark, cut out or in a soft vignette; archival photos keep their grain, set in a gold hairline mount.
- Wide margins and centred hero objects are allowed here (ceremony is symmetrical); text sits beside the object, not over it.

## Signature motion
- Slow tempo (1.3 s, blooms 2 s, stagger 220 ms): `fade` and `zoom` for the object (bloom_ms 2 s for the slow ones), `wipe` along gold lines.
- Signature: the instrument strings as three graduated lines that live in the margin and become the timeline (Morph).
- A ring medallion in three layers that rotates and blooms at the chapter turn.

## Avoid
bright grounds, more than one accent, cartoon icons, fast entrances, lacquer and gold on modern technology or data (it turns them into antiques).
