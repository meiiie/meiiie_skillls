# noir-product — near-black stage, one amber signal

**Use for** product launches, hardware, aerospace and developer tools: one object or screen at a time, lit on a dark stage.
**Palette** ground `#0A0B0D`, second ground `#121419`, surface `#171A1F`, ink `#E9ECF1`, muted `#8E96A3`, hairline `#2A2F37`, accent `#FFB020`, second voice `#6EE7F9` (from the pack, assets/styles/noir-product.json).
**Fonts** Space Grotesk (display) · Be Vietnam Pro (text) · JetBrains Mono (labels).
**Routing fit** (route.py reads it from the pack): domains technology 1, aerospace 0.8, engineering 0.6, software 0.6, business 0.4, energy 0.3; tones cinematic, bold, precise, technical.

## Layout grammar
- Near-black ground, a slightly lifted second ground for screens; ONE amber signal (`accent`) per slide, cyan only as a second data series.
- Space Grotesk display, Be Vietnam Pro text, JetBrains Mono kickers and specs (`!!mark`, `!!rule`).
- The product cut out or photographed on black, big and off-centre; specs set as mono label + large numeral beside it.
- Screens sit in a bezel (`screen`), soft long shadows only under the hero object.

## Signature motion
- 1.1 s tempo, 180 ms stagger; `zoom` reveal for the hero, `wipe` for the light beam, Morph between part states.
- Signature: one light beam (a 2 pt accent line) scans each screen and becomes the progress rail.
- The block cursor: a small amber square that blinks, grows into a panel and reveals the product inside.

## Avoid
warm paper grounds, several accents, heritage or food subjects (a black stage reads as a gadget launch and makes food clinical).

## Routing rejects it when the deck is about
- heritage: a near-black product stage with one amber signal is a gadget launch
- food: a black stage with one cold signal makes food look clinical
