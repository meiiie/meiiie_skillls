# art-analysis.md - details (moved from principles/ in v6.22)
- **Separate meaningful from decorative.** A trait is meaningful when it comes from the subject:
  kraft paper and polaroids for a case file, a scroll and ink wash for classical thought. It is a
  cliché when it is applied to anything: gingham, stickers, glow frames, diamond masks, photo-filled
  letters, grunge.
- **Turn findings into decisions.** Write each one as "cause -> what I will do -> which slides".
  Put the list in design/directions.md, before three-directions.
- **Re-run the questions on your contact sheet** in look-then-fix. Any slide that fails two or more
  questions gets redone.
