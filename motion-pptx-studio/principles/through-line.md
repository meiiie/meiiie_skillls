# through-line
Duarte: the story is built before the slides; Apple: one idea per slide. A deck of good slides with no spine
scores 7.
- **Before the storyboard, write design/story.md**: the through-line in one sentence (what the audience
  believes at the end that they did not at the start), then the arc (what is -> what could be swings, a turn,
  a call), then one line per slide.
- **Every slide states its purpose in one line** - what the audience must get and feel - in story.md, in deck.json
  `"purpose"` and in its notes. The image, the type and the keyframe sequence all serve that line.
- A slide whose purpose is missing, vague or duplicates a neighbour is cut or merged (the critic orders CUT/REWORK).
- The signature device (concept-art-direction) carries the through-line visually.
