# photo-treatment
Treatment makes photos from different cameras look like one world, and makes each one sit in the slide's material.
- **Grade per chapter.** Use one grade or duotone per chapter (`surface.py photo --duotone`/`--grade`). Archival,
  street and farm photos then share one light.
- **Shared skin.** Give photos and the ground the same grain. A clean photo on a grainy ground looks pasted on.
- **Light falloff for type.** Darken or lighten the photo toward the side where the type sits. Don't use a box
  behind the text. Body text needs 4.5:1 contrast, checked in greyscale.
- **Edges come from the material.** A print border with a soft shadow on paper, a deckled or torn edge, a
  cut-out's contact shadow. No generic drop shadow or rounded card.
- **Crop for the telling detail.** Crop to hands, a stencil or a rim, not to the whole scene. Crop hard and
  let it bleed.
- **Honest edits.** Credit the photo on the slide and say "(đã chỉnh sửa)" or "(tách nền)" when it was graded or
  cut out (credit-every-image). Never composite photos into a scene that never happened.
