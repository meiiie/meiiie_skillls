# natural-vietnamese
Write as a Vietnamese editor would, not as a translation.
- Numbers: decimal comma and thousands dot (4,25 năm; 27.000 năm; 2,5 triệu); units
  spaced (8 phút 20 giây).
- Prefer Vietnamese terms with the international one once in brackets: dịch chuyển đỏ
  (redshift).
- Short, concrete titles that state the finding ("Mặt Trời bạn thấy đã cũ 8 phút"), not
  topics ("Về Mặt Trời").
- The script reads aloud at 160-190 syllables per minute; one notes block per slide,
  40-120 syllables.
- Check diacritics in every font and film (Text caching and font fallback break "ờ", "ữ"
  first).
