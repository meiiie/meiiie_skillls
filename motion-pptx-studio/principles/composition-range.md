# composition-range
Variety comes from a range you decide in advance. Random layouts are not variety. Set the range before the storyboard.
- **Name 5-7 compositions for this deck** in ds.py, each with a job. Draw them as wireframes on the board.
  At least two must be photo arrangements (photo-arrangement) other than a split.
- **Move the anchor.** The title is not in the same place on most slides. Rotate among top-left, low-centre, a
  vertical rail, over the image, and behind a cut-out.
- **Rhythm.** No more than 2 neighbours share a composition. Alternate dense and airy, light and dark, big and small.
  A chapter opener breaks the grid.
- **Art density, checked at 1x.** Each chapter has at least one bold full-bleed photo or a dark dramatic slide.
  The material is visible at contact-sheet size. Never a small icon, drawing or cup in a large empty field:
  scale it to hero size or cut the slide.
- **Scale range 15-25x** between the biggest and smallest type. One slide per chapter has an element that fills
  2/3 of the frame.
- **Recolour test.** If swapping the colours of two slides makes them the same slide, they are one composition.
- `scripts/art_check.py renders/rN --pptx DECK` warns on runs of one composition. It is crude, so look yourself.
