# concept-art-direction
Studios decide the idea before the look (Pentagram: "moodboards kill ideas"; Duarte: discovery, then ONE
direction from three unique solutions). Our decks stalled at 7-7.7 because directions differed in palette, not idea.
- **Signature device first.** One visual idea that runs through the whole deck and could belong to no other
  topic: "everything seen through a container door", "the token as a physical tile", "Tết counted in hands".
  Write it in design/treatment.md as one sentence plus the real object/photo that carries it.
- **One-page treatment:** signature device; ONE image treatment (grade, grain, crop logic, edge); type voice;
  light/dark arc across chapters; 3 signature moments (slide, what the eye sees, which technique from
  references/techniques.md).
- **Three directions, A deliberately extreme** (Pentagram "temperature check": the boldest version you can
  imagine), B editorial/magazine, C cinematic/dark. Each its own device reading, photos, layouts.
- The pick critic rejects any direction without a signature device or one that is a recolour of another.
- Moodboards only illustrate the chosen concept; never start from them.
- The device shows up in every chapter opener and in at least 1/3 of slides, varied in scale and crop - a
  thread, not a stamp (repetition of one identical picture still fails REUSE).
