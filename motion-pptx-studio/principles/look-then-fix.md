# look-then-fix
You do not know what you made until you have looked at it.
- After every film render: a contact strip (5+ frames). After every deck build: the
  contact sheets of ALL slides.
- Look for: overlaps, clipped text, empty halves, type below 10 pt, two slides that look
  the same, a film whose first second is blank, sources running into the film.
- Score each film and the deck 1-10 in critique-log.md (concept, film, composition,
  type, facts) with ONE reason each.
- Anything under 8 gets one redo loop before delivery. Write what changed.
- Scores are your own and therefore generous: say so, and name the weakest slide
  honestly.
