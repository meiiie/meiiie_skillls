# examples-are-a-yardstick-not-a-mold
The decks in examples/ show a level of care. They are not a look to reuse.
- **Never copy** an example's palette, fonts, motif (rails, postmarks, isobars), layouts or
  chapter structure. Two decks on different topics must not look related.
- **Do match their care:**
  - a concept with a reason behind every colour and font;
  - a background on every slide and a picture for every hero number;
  - mandatory films;
  - a board critiqued before building;
  - honest scores.
- **Invent a fresh direction per topic:** start from the subject's own material
  (fit-style-to-subject), sketch 2-3 directions, then choose one.
- **No house style.** The scripts fall back to neutral greyscale and the ManimGX default faces
  only so a missing choice is obvious. Bundled style JSON is labelled optional and illustration
  only. Every deck defines its palette and fonts in its own ds.py / deck.json.
- **Compare against the yardstick at the end.** Put your contact sheet next to
  examples/reference-trai-dat/contact.jpg. Wherever yours is plainer, it is not finished.
