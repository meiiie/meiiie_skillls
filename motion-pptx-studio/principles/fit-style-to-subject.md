# fit-style-to-subject
The style comes from the subject's own material culture, not from a theme list.
- Economy as weather -> synoptic-chart paper, isobars, vermilion and Prussian blue.
  Light as mail -> airmail paper, postmarks, red/blue chevrons. Find the object people
  of that field actually handle.
- 6-8 colours, each with ONE job written next to it (ground, ink, secondary, the two
  forces, one highlight).
- Three or four families, one role each (voice, display numerals, mono metadata, body),
  chosen for this concept. They must cover the language's diacritics: test-render "Tiếng Việt: ằ ẳ ẵ ặ
  ữ ự ỹ" before committing.
- Put the tokens in one ds.py imported by films AND the deck builder, so film and slide
  match exactly.
