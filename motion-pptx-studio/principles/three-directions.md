# three-directions
Every use starts with three art directions that differ radically, never one obvious palette.
Variety comes from the topic, the audience and the topic's own imagery. It never comes from a
house style or from the examples.
- **Write three directions in design/directions.md.** For each one, give:
  - a name;
  - a source world (a real object, trade or medium from the topic: a ledger, a sack stencil,
    a radio dial, a herbarium sheet, a market sign, a patent drawing);
  - a palette of 4-6 colours with a job per colour;
  - a type pairing;
  - a motif;
  - a background strategy;
  - the film language: ManimGX camera, Anatomy machine or web-film look.
- **Make them radically different.** Vary at least three of these between directions: light or
  dark ground, colour temperature, serif or grotesque or slab voice, photo-led or
  illustration-led, dense or airy, archival or contemporary.
- **Reject the cliché first.** Write the obvious look down (coffee = cream and brown, space = navy
  and stars, finance = blue and green) and do not choose it unless you can say why it is right.
