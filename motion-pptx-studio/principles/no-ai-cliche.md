# no-ai-cliche
The bar is FT / Bloomberg / The Economist / Pentagram, not a template gallery.
Banned: starfield or nebula backgrounds behind text, glowing particles, neon gradients,
glassmorphism cards,
stock "handshake/rocket/lightbulb" icons, three-icon bullet rows, generic AI-generated
people or planets,
the same big-number stat card repeated on 4+ slides, emoji.
Instead: real evidence (photographs and documents with credits), a drawn instrument, a
chart that is the point.
Photographs are mounted like prints on the page (a mat, a caption, a credit), never
wallpapered under text.
Ask of every slide: would a design editor recognise this layout from a thousand other
decks? Then redo it.
