# credit-every-image
Use real images with an open licence and say whose they are.
- Sources: NASA / ESA / NOAA / museum open access, Wikimedia Commons (PD, CC0, CC BY, CC
  BY-SA). `fetch_commons.py search|keep` records licence + author into
  assets/CREDITS.md.
- Verify the subject by eye; a title is not proof. Use the agency's own caption for what
  it shows.
- On the slide: a short credit line in the source position ("Ảnh: NASA/JPL-Caltech, phạm
  vi công cộng").
- Artist's impressions are labelled as such ("hình minh hoạ của NASA").
- CC BY-SA images keep their licence name in the credits slide.
