# principles-used
Every deliverable ends with a "Principles used" section (in NOTES.md): one line per
decision,
`principle -> decision it shaped -> where (slide/film)`. Example:
- film-is-the-core -> route 1 (Manim available), 4 films, each with a native end-state
  slide -> 05/06, 14/15 ...
- never-invent-numbers -> dropped "2 trillion galaxies" (sources disagree) -> slide 12
A principle you cannot point to a decision for was not used; do not list it. A decision
with no principle
behind it is a candidate to cut.
