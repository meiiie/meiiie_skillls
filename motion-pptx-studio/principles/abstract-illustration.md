# abstract-illustration
Abstract topics (money, AI, privacy, policy) still have bodies. The R5-R10 abstract decks sank to 6.5-7 on
cream number cards, arrows and fake app screens. A hero slide is never a card.
- **Every abstract hero slide is one of three things, nothing else:**
  1. **A metaphor-object photo from the library** - the concept as a physical thing (inflation: a price board;
     blockchain: a ledger book, a mining rig; AI: a GPU board, a hand on a keyboard at night), full-bleed or
     a big cut-out, the claim set in the quiet area.
  2. **A typographic poster** - one word or number >= 250 pt, cropped by the frame, set ON a photo or a
     visible material (paper, metal, fabric), one small annotation. Not on a flat card.
  3. **A lit ManimGX 3-D still** - a surface, field or stack of solids with real light and shadow.
- **Diagrams only for mechanism** (how a block links, how a model predicts): in the middle of a chapter, drawn
  with the deck's materials, never as a hero and never two in a row.
- **Mock app screens: at most 1 per deck**, and only when that app IS the subject. A screen is not an image of an idea.
- **Card limit (art_check CARDS):** a card = slide with no real photo, no film, no cut-out, no type >= 150 pt
  (sources slide excluded); a deck fails with more than max(2, slides/6). Turn each card into 1-3 above.
- **Banned as a default:** isometric mint/pastel blocks, flat rounded boxes with icons, cube cities. An isometric
  scene only when it is the real structure, with materials and light, max one per chapter.
- No photo at all only when nothing physical exists: deck.json "no_photo_reason", then posters and 3-D stills.
