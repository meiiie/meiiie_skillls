# one-concept
One visual world carries the whole deck; every chapter turns ONE of its numbers into ONE
instrument.
- Write the concept as a sentence a stranger could repeat: "every sky photo is a letter;
  the postmark is when its light left."
- Map it: chapter -> the number(s) it must land -> the instrument that shows them -> the
  film. If a chapter has no instrument, the concept is a frame, not a concept (the
  "herbarium plates" deck: pretty labels, nothing measured).
- The metaphor must DO work: its parts are axes, scales or mechanisms of the data, not
  decoration.
- Cover, agenda and close reuse the same object (Morph it like a camera) so the deck
  reads as one piece.
Test: cover the titles. Can you still tell which chapter you are in, and what was
measured?
