# one-type-scale
Seven sizes on every slide and in every film, nothing else: 10 / 13 / 17 / 23 / 34 / 58
/ 132 pt
(source, label, body, sub, title, display, hero). Films convert pt to pixels at their
on-slide scale.
- One grid: 12 columns, 0.6 in margins, the film window x 0.6, y 1.62, w 12.13, h 5.12
  in.
- Fixed chrome: kicker + title + standfirst top, source line at y 6.77 and page number
  bottom - same place every slide.
- Body text <= 40 words a slide; the rest is the speaker's job (the notes).
- A label never sits on top of a line, a stamp or another label. Check the renders, not
  the JSON.
Mít test had 23 sizes; the flagship had 7. That alone separated "a deck" from "a
publication".
