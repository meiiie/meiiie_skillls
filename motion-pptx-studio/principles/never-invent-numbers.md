# never-invent-numbers
Every number on a slide, in a film or in the script traces to a row of research/facts.md
(id | fact | source | date).
- Search the primary source (agency, paper, statistics office) and write down the date
  you read it.
- A derived number says how: "1,3 s = 384.400 km / c (derived)". Projections say "dự
  báo".
- Sources disagree? Say so, or use neither ("at least 100 billion" vs "2 trillion"
  galaxies).
- Illustrations that are not data say so on the slide ("hình minh hoạ, không theo tỉ
  lệ").
- No number is better than a plausible-looking guess. Cut the claim rather than soften a
  source.
