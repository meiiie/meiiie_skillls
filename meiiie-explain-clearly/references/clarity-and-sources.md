# Clarity scope and primary sources

## Use the influence honestly

ASD-STE100 is a controlled English standard for technical documentation. It contains writing rules and a controlled dictionary; its rules also permit domain-specific technical nouns and verbs. It is more specific than ordinary plain language. Short sentences alone do not establish conformance.

Use the transferable principles for teaching: clear actions, consistent terms, manageable sentence structure, and logical ordering. Treat the learner's “80%” request as a strength-of-style preference, not a quantitative test. Do not label Vietnamese writing “official STE.” Use natural Vietnamese with analogous clarity principles; do not apply English dictionary membership or English word-count rules to Vietnamese.

For a formal conformance request, identify the applicable standard issue, obtain lawful access to it, and use the actual rules and dictionary with a qualified review appropriate to the task. If these are unavailable, report the limit and offer an explicitly STE-inspired edit. Do not claim certification, endorsement, or compliance merely because an AI wrote or checked the text.

Do not bundle or reproduce the full standard, controlled dictionary, or third-party infographic. Link official sources and create original examples. Preserve source/license information for any assets actually reused. The user-supplied inspirational quote has not been independently authenticated; its attribution is not a skill premise.
