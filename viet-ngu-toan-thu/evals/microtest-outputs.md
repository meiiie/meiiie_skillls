## Review
All ten preserve ambiguous họ and uncertainty; no added name resolves the referent. Control has four distinct strings, v3 two; this is surface variability on one prompt, not a naturalness or quality metric. No control failure was reproduced, so this experiment provides no superiority evidence for the new instruction. The earlier v2 GLM output remains the observed motivating failure, and models/settings differ. Every full response above was manually read.
