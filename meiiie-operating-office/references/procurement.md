# Procurement delivery

## Lock the purchasing specification

Capture purpose, exact required specifications, quantity, destination, deadline, currency, spending ceiling, condition, warranty, seller constraints, and whether substitutes are acceptable. Ask only for missing requirements that change the shortlist. Do not assume compatibility or exchangeability from similar product names.

Use useful roles selectively: specification/compatibility checker, supplier researcher, commercial comparison analyst, or purchase verifier. Keep purchasing authority with the authorized user decision and applicable tool approval process.

## Verify comparable offers

For each viable offer record:

- Manufacturer, full model/SKU or part number, exact variant, capacity, size, color, regional version, included accessories, and quantity
- Seller identity, listing URL, seller-of-record where visible, condition (new, refurbished, used), stock claim, and delivery estimate
- Price and currency, VAT/tax treatment, mandatory fees, shipping, discounts and their conditions, and landed total to the stated destination where available
- Warranty, returns/cancellation restrictions, and relevant seller credibility evidence
- Observation date/time and what remains unverified

Verify matching variants before ranking by price. Distinguish a listing's “in stock” claim from confirmed checkout availability. Do not label an offer new, VAT-inclusive, available, compatible, or cheapest without supporting evidence. Record unavailable tax, shipping, or delivery details explicitly; do not silently compare incomplete totals as equivalent.

Prefer fewer verified options over filling a quota with mismatches, duplicates, stale listings, or questionable suppliers. If only two options meet a request for five, return those two and explain the search gap. Separate exact matches from authorized alternatives.

## Recommend and prepare action

Rank against the user's actual priorities: fit, total cost, delivery reliability, warranty, returns, and supplier risk. Explain tradeoffs and time-sensitive uncertainty. Recheck material price, stock, variant, quantity, and total near purchase.

Comparison or cart preparation is not purchase approval. Before committing, obtain the required approval for the actual item, seller, all-in amount/currency, payment method, one-time or recurring commitment, destination/service method, and visible material restrictions. Escalate changed totals, substitutions, subscriptions, or other material terms beyond the approved scope. Do not complete unsupported payment or authentication actions.

After an authorized purchase, verify the actual confirmation and report item, quantity, total, order status, and delivery expectations without exposing payment secrets. Do not claim an order was placed because a cart or checkout page exists.
